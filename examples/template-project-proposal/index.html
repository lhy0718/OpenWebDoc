Regional partner enablement pilot

A proposal format for explaining the business need, pilot scope, operating model, success criteria, and decision path for a cross-functional project.

NeedRegional partners need a consistent onboarding and support motion.
ApproachRun a six-week enablement pilot with shared playbooks.
ValidationMeasure activation, support load, and partner confidence.
OutcomeDecide whether to scale the partner program next quarter.

Phase Deliverable Review question
