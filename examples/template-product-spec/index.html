Product specification

Customer insight inbox spec

A structured product spec for turning support tickets, sales notes, and research quotes into prioritized product insights with clear ownership and review gates.

Problem

Customer feedback is scattered across tools, making repeated themes hard to verify.

Non-goals

No automatic roadmap commitment, sentiment scoring, or customer-facing reply drafting.

Success

Product managers can trace each insight to source notes before adding it to planning.

Functional requirements

Capability User behavior Acceptance rule
Collect sources Import ticket excerpts, call notes, and research quotes into one intake queue. Every insight links to at least one source item.
Cluster themes Group related evidence by workflow, account segment, and severity. Theme cards show count, examples, and confidence.
Review actions Assign an owner, decision date, and next artifact for each accepted theme. Unassigned themes stay out of roadmap reports.
OwnerProduct operations
Release riskMedium until source-link review
FallbackExport a manual insight digest
